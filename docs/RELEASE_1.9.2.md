# Wheel: v1.9.2

iPhone **1.9.2 (8)** · Web **1.9.2** · Branch **`main`**

## Trade review

Trade review opens on the current month’s trading days. Choose **Year** to see realized P&L by month or **All time** to see it by year. Tap a year, month, or day to inspect its detail. All time shows the dates covered by archived Flex reports. P&L stays in the selected currency, and commissions retain their reported currencies. [#200](https://github.com/WahBun/AllYouNeedIsWheel/pull/200)

## Charts and Settings

- During an iPhone chart refresh, a broker entry price no longer flashes as a new Buy or Sell draft. Superchart shows favorites sync errors in the Favorites menu and keeps the selected contract and order feedback visible. [#199](https://github.com/WahBun/AllYouNeedIsWheel/pull/199)
- In **Settings → Access & Feedback**, the Gateway row shows the running IB Gateway version next to its connection status when Mini can verify the local container and port. [#201](https://github.com/WahBun/AllYouNeedIsWheel/pull/201)

## Updating

Deploy from **main** on Mini and refresh the web page. For an upgrade from the November 14 release, choose **Deploy & Restart Backend** to load the new reporting and maintenance endpoints. The deployment and Pro Xcode sync tools now use main. [#198](https://github.com/WahBun/AllYouNeedIsWheel/pull/198)

Build and Run Wheel from the release commit to update the iPhone. Settings shows **1.9.2 (8)** after installation.
